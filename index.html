A More Responsible Vision of Mining

Traditional cryptocurrency mining can require large amounts of electricity and specialized hardware.


BANK 2.0 MINING wants users to discover the idea of mining from another perspective: a simple cloud-based reward experience focused on accessibility, education and environmental responsibility.


The goal is not to turn your mobile phone into a mining machine. The goal is to show that blockchain technology can continue evolving while exploring more efficient and responsible models.

GET STARTED

Start in a Few Steps

You only need a Solana-compatible wallet address. No complicated configuration is required.

1

Choose a Wallet

Use a compatible wallet such as BANK 2.0 - WALLET, Trust Wallet, MetaMask or Phantom.

2

Get Your Solana Address

Create a Solana account inside your wallet, or copy your existing Solana public address.

3

Save Your Address

Paste your public Solana wallet address inside BANK 2.0 MINING and press Save.

4

Start

Start the mining experience. Each completed 12-hour cycle can generate a BANK 2.0 reward.

Never enter your seed phrase, private key or wallet password. BANK 2.0 MINING only requires your PUBLIC Solana wallet address to identify where eligible rewards can be sent.

Every 12 Hours. One New Opportunity.

Keep participating, complete your mining cycles and become part of the growing BANK 2.0 community.
